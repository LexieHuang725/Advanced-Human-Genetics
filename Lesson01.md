# Lesson01 - Case Study
> A 68 y/o assigned female at birth (AFAB), Mrs. Jones, is referred to
genetic counseling by her breast surgeon after having a biopsy of her
left breast. The pathology results identified ductal carcinoma in situ
(DCIS), a non-invasive form of breast cancer. Her doctor is very
concerned because this is the second cancer diagnosis for Mrs. Jones.
She was diagnosed with and treated for papillary serous ovarian cancer
with a total abdominal hysterectomy and bilateral salpingo-
oophorectomy, followed by chemotherapy, at the age of 48. In 2003,
due to her personal history of ovarian cancer and family history of breast
cancer, her oncologist ordered BRCA1/2 testing that was negative.

1. List the “red flags” for hereditary cancer present in Mrs. Jones’ personal medical history.

2. Generate a list of questions to ask Mrs.Jones during the appointment, especially regarding her family history (i.e. what additional information will help you to formulate a probable diagnosis?)

3. Review the family history form that Mrs. Jones completed in the waiting room. Then, use this information to complete the pedigree provided.

4. List the “red flags” for hereditary cancer present in the family history

> Additional family history details:
> 
> Paternal side (Irish ancestry): father died at 51 from a car accident. He had 3 brothers and 2
sisters, all of whom are deceased except for one sister. A brother had lung cancer at the age
of 60 and a sister had ovarian cancer in her 80s. Father’s mother died of stomach cancer in
her 70s and his father died of stomach cancer in his 60s.
> 
> Maternal side (French/Italian ancestry): mother died at age 56 from colon cancer, dx at age 52. She had 1 brother who is healthy. Her mother died during childbirth at 23 and her father
died of heart disease at 65. It is reported that mom’s paternal female cousin had breast
cancer.
> 
> Mrs. Jones’s Siblings: Her sister, Jean, died of breast cancer at age 58, dx age 54. This sister

Mrs. Jones’s Children: She has a healthy daughter, age 43. Her son was diagnosed with
