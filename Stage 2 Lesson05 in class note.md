## When to order a karyotype
* Patients with obvious chromosomal syndrome (eg. Trisomy 21)
* Family history of chromosomal rearrangement
* History of multiple miscarriage
* Prenatal diagnosis of aneuploidy

## When to order a FISH
* Rapid diagnosis of aneuploidy (trisomy 21, 13, X, Y) or triploidy
* If a specific microdeletion/duplication syndrome is suspected (eg. 22q11.2 deletion syndrome)
* To identify the origin of marker or translocated chr

### Limitation of FISH
* You need to have a region of interest in mind
* Does not distinguish trisomy due to nondisjunction vs. chromosome rearrangement
* Not efficient for genome-wide

## When to order array
* (ACMG) First line test in initial postnatal evaluation of those with
  * Multiple congenital anomalies and no known syndrome
  * developmental delay/intellectual disability
  * ASD
* (ACOG) recommended for pregnancy w one or more major structural anomalies
   on ultrasound and who is undergoing invasive prenatal diagnosis

## Chromosomal microarray
### Array CGH
* Array-based comparative genome hybridization
* Looking for CNV
* **Compared to control**

### SNP Aray
* Looks at variation in nucleotide bases among individuals
* **No control required**

### Limitation of arrays
* Can't detect balanced rearrangment
* Potential to detect VUS
* Probe spacing and reslution

## Genetic markers
* DNA sequence with known physical location on a chr
* may/may not be part of a gene or have no known function
* Types
  - RFLPs: *restriction fragment length polymorphisms*; Southern Blot or PCR
  - VNTRs: *variable number tandem repeats (microsatellites)*; automatic multiples PCR
  - SNPs: *single nucleotide polymorphisms*; microarray

## Linkage Analysis
* technique used for DNA mapping/discovery
* Tandenvy for gene that are close tgt on a chr to be inherited tgt during meiosis
* Genes near each other are less likely to be separated by crossing over/recomb and are "linked"
  - completely linked = recomb frequency of 0
  - unlinked = recomb frequency of 0.5

## Unlinked
* Independent Assortment:
  - 50% parental (nonrecomb) chr
  - 50% nonparental (recomb) chr
* Linkage is a dparture from independent assortment
