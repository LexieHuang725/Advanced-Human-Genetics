## Contiguous Gene Syndrome
| Main category | What changed | Common subtypes |
|---|---|---|
| **Numerical chromosome abnormalities** | The number of chromosomes | **Aneuploidy** (extra or missing chromosome, such as trisomy 21 or Turner syndrome); **polyploidy** (extra whole chromosome sets) |
| **Structural chromosome abnormalities** | The structure or amount of chromosome material | **Deletion**, **duplication**, **translocation**, **inversion**, and **ring chromosome** |

> Structural chromosomal abnormalities → copy-number changes → deletions or duplications → contiguous gene syndromes
>
* **Clinical Feature**: developmental delay, intellectual disability, dysmorphic features, birth defects/organ malformations
* attribute to extra/deficient copies of multiple contiguous genes within deleted/duplicated region
* mechanism:
  - sporadic
  - due to aberrant recombination

* What is the chance that future pregnancies will be

  - The next step is to test both biological parents for the deletion:
    - If a parent carries the deletion: each pregnancy has a 50% chance of inheriting it. The severity in an affected child cannot be predicted from the parent’s experience.

## 22q11 Deletion Syndrome
### Clinical Features
* Heart malformations (esp. conotruncal)
* kidney abnormalities, GI problems, skeletal differences
* Thymic and parathyroid hypoplasia
* Immunodeficiency
* Low levels of calcium (can result in seizures)
* Cleft palate
* Hearing loss
* DD, learning difficulties and mental illness (esp. schizophrenia)
* Typical facies

<img width="710" height="335" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/10d88143-ae4f-4363-819c-eca801331ec9" />

### Prevalence
* 1 in 4,000 live births
  - **Most common microdeletion syndrome**
  - Usually sporadic, but individuals can reproduce and it follows AD pattern
* Region is flanked by low-copy number repeats (LCRs) that
occur frequently throughout the genome (LCRs are DNA sequences that are highly similar to other sequences nearby. Around 22q11.2, these repeated sequences make it harder for chromosomes to pair up precisely during meiosis -> lead to mismatch.)
  - Can lead to misalignment during meiosis

## Del 1p36.3 Syndrome
* **Similar frequency to del (22q)
syndrome**, 1/5,000
* Emerged through the improved
cytogenetic techniques and use
of *FISH* in the 1990s
* Hypotonia, microcephaly,
growth delay, severe learning
difficulties, epilepsy
* Characteristically straight
eyebrows w/slightly deep-set
eyes and midface hypoplasia
* **Majority de novo, 95%**

## Williams Syndrome (7q11.23 ELN deletion)
### Clinical Features
* “Elfin-like” facial appearance
* “Cocktail party manner” in
childhood - the child can master the superficial mechanics of a casual "cocktail party" conversation, but they struggle with deep comprehension, spatial reasoning, and meaningful structural interaction.
* Intellectual disability
* Endocrine Abnormalities
### Management
* Early intervention/special edu programs and
vocational training address developmental
disabilities
  - Speech/language, physical, occupational, feeding
and sensory integration therapies
* Psychological evaluation and treatment especially
for ADD and anxiety
* Surgery may be required for SVAS
* Treat other manifestations as you would in the
general population

* Etiologies:

* Congenital heart defects
* Distinct facial appearance
* Microcephaly
* Round face

* Etiologies:

  - Unbalanced translocations (almost half)

* Microcephaly, Hypertelorism,
High arched eyebrows, Broad

Dysplastic ears with pits/tags,
